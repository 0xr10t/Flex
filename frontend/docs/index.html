Overview

Flex is a terminal app that lets you pay people by typing what you want. A model on your own machine works out the plan, plain code checks every value, you approve it, and MetaMask signs it. 

send 50 usdc to aradhya on base and 20 to mihir on arbitrum

Flex works out who you mean, which chain your money is on, and whether it has to bridge first. Then it shows you a summary. Nothing moves until you say yes, and MetaMask asks you once more before anything is signed.

How a request flows

  1. You type a request into flexx.
  2. Flex refreshes your balances and stops right there if you pasted a private key or seed phrase.
  3. The model pulls out amounts and recipients. Code checks them and resolves names through your contacts and ENS.
  4. If something is missing or unclear, Flex asks you instead of guessing.
  5. Code builds the plan, including source chains, bridges and schedules, and checks it against your balances and limits.
  6. You get a plain summary and answer y or n.
  7. On yes, the wallet simulates the transactions, then MetaMask asks you to sign.

What runs where

Everything runs on your machine. The model runs through Ollama, your data sits in encrypted local databases, and chain reads, ENS lookups and web fetches go out through Tor by default. There's no Flex server and no cloud AI.

What's supported

Flex runs on Ethereum Sepolia, Base Sepolia and Arbitrum Sepolia, with USDC and ETH. It doesn't do swaps, NFTs, arbitrary contract calls or mainnet.
