GUIDES

Using Flex

Talk to it the way you'd ask a friend to pay someone for you.

send 50 usdc to aradhya on base
send 10 usdc to everyone under iitr.eth
what's my balance on arbitrum?
did my last payment go through?

Paying people

You can pay an address, a saved contact or an ENS name, and several people in one go. Payments on the same chain go out as one atomic batch. If your USDC is on the wrong chain, Flex adds a Chainlink CCIP bridge step, with a cap on the fee.

For an ENS group such as iitr.eth, Flex lists the subnames and you pick who gets paid. If a name could mean more than one person, it asks.

Approving

Every payment ends in a plain summary that you answer with y or n. On yes, the wallet opens an approval page in your browser and MetaMask asks you to sign that exact plan.

The first payment on each network also asks you to switch your MetaMask account to a smart account (EIP-7702). The approval page walks you through it, and it costs a little test ETH.

Commands

Type / to open the command menu, and ? for keyboard shortcuts.

/balance
Your balances on every chain.
/contacts
Read or edit saved contacts and the networks they use.
/limits
Show your spending limits, or change one. Nothing typed into the chat itself can change them.
/cancel
Drop the request that's waiting on you.
/wallet
Open the wallet page to approve in MetaMask.
/get and /db
Read your local records, such as transactions or recurring plans, without changing anything.
/status and /doctor
Check that everything is running, and get told how to fix what isn't.
/copy
Copy the last reply.
/new
Start a fresh session.

Ctrl+C clears what you've typed, and pressing it twice on an empty line exits. When you leave, Flex stops the services it started. Ollama and Tor keep running for next time.
