Updating and removing

Your settings and data live inside the install, so keep a copy before you replace it.

Updating

There's no in-place update yet. Save your settings and data, reinstall, and put them back:

cp -a ~/.flex/current/.env ~/.flex/current/.local ~/flex-backup/
rm -rf ~/.flex
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/0xr10t/Flex/master/install.sh | bash
cp -a ~/flex-backup/.env ~/flex-backup/.local ~/.flex/current/

Make the backup folder first with mkdir ~/flex-backup. .env holds your settings, and .local holds your contacts, history and configs.

Removing

rm -rf ~/.flex

Flexx also keeps its database key in your system keychain, under the service agent-layer. You can delete that entry too. Nothing else on your system was changed.
